THE BUSINESS MODEL

I would not build Castle Eden around room revenue alone.

Your website should quietly communicate that the property has several premium revenue streams:

Revenue Stream	Positioning
Heritage room stays	Premium nightly rate
Entire-house booking	High-ticket exclusive stay
Private dining	Premium per-person experience
Cultural experiences	Paid add-on
Workshops	Ticketed experiences
Private events	Venue + food + experience
Corporate retreats	High-value packages
Film/photo shoots	Location fee
Membership	Recurring revenue
Special cultural evenings	Ticketed premium events

The important idea is:

One property → multiple premium experiences → multiple revenue streams.

PREMIUM PACKAGES

Rather than publishing a long list of cheap packages, create three levels.

THE HERITAGE STAY

For the traveller who wants the Castle Eden experience.

Stay + breakfast + personalised local recommendations

THE EDEN EXPERIENCE

For guests who want more than accommodation.

Stay + private dining + heritage experience + curated city exploration

THE CASTLE EXCLUSIVE

For guests who want complete privacy.

Exclusive residence + personalised hospitality + private dining + curated experiences + dedicated itinerary

Price: By enquiry

For the highest-end package, I would deliberately avoid displaying the price online. Use an enquiry process so your team can understand the guest, occasion, group size and requirements before quoting.
